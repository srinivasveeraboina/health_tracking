Alarms: The web app uses the Notifications and Service Worker APIs. On many platforms, alarms will fire while the app is open or in background, but may not reliably trigger when the browser is fully closed. For guaranteed alarms when the app is closed, consider wrapping this app with a native shell (Capacitor/Cordova) for access to native alarm APIs.
